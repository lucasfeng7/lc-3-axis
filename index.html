An Accurate Three-Axis Force Sensor Assembled with Readily Available Off-the-Shelf Components with No Designed Compliant Element

Lucas Feng, Ariel Slepyan, Krishna Murthy, Nitish Thakor
Johns Hopkins University
IROS 2026 Workshop on Sensors and Actuators for Dexterous Manipulation
Paper Repository CAD Firmware

Admittance hand-guiding on a UR5e at 199.8 Hz — the operator pushes, the sensor measures, the arm follows.

Assembled, and open — four load cells forming the structure, converters on the perimeter, multiplexer at the center.

Calibration against known masses, and validation in series with a commercial OptoForce reference on the UR5e.

Four off-the-shelf load cells carry the entire load path, with no custom compliant element.
